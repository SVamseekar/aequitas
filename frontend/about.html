UK Bus Transport Policy Intelligence

Aequitas is a policy intelligence platform for UK government, Local Transport Authorities, and transport researchers. It pre-computes evidence-graded analytics across 8 policy dimensions, covering all 33,755 LSOAs in England.

8 Policy Dimensions

  • Equity & Deprivation — Gini coefficient, Lorenz curve, Palma ratio, deprivation indices
  • Accessibility — 2SFCA gaps to jobs, NHS facilities, and schools
  • Service Quality — headway analysis, evening isolation, Sunday deserts
  • Route Network — BODS routes, operator concentration, cross-LA coverage
  • Modal Shift & Carbon — DfT elasticity estimates and DESNZ emission factors
  • Economic Appraisal — BCR via TAG v2.03fc, Green Book NPV
  • Bus Services Act 2025 — LTA franchising readiness tiers
  • Policy Scenarios — frequency restoration, last bus extension, DRT coverage
